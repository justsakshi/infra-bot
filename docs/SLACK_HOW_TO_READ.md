# How to read the bot (pin this in the channels)

Every post starts with one of three signs:

- ✅ Nothing to do.
- ⚠️ Look at it today.
- 🚨 Act now: something is still sending from a bad inbox, a key is broken, or money is about to be lost.

The first lines say what happened and the numbers that matter. The details are in the thread under the post: click **"N replies"**. Work from the thread, so the team can see who did what.

**Kill warnings (Tuesdays):** an inbox should come out of campaigns after two bad placement tests at least 7 days apart, one test under 50% on 20+ seeds, or when its whole domain is under 80% over 40+ seeds. Take it out of campaigns first, then retire or replace it at the next bill (`/domains`, then *Before the bill*).

**Retire vs check (before a bill):** *Retire* inboxes failed a recent test, sit on a spam domain, or aren't in Smartlead, so stop paying for them. *Check* inboxes have no recent test, a broken connection or low warmup, so look before deciding.

**Stopping a payment:** Zapmail inboxes are retired one by one and disappear at the next bill. ScaledMail bills per order, so either replace a domain or cancel the whole order.

**Buying:** `/domains`, then *Suggest*, tick names, then *Buy these*. Staging is free. Only red buttons (*Place order*, *Approve & start*) spend money, and only approvers can press them.
